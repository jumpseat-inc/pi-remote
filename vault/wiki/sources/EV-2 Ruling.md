Four rulings on EV-2 (2026-08-31). **Item 1** — the 403 row names `/rc:login` AND a distinct admin/scope clause, establishing the test that a remedy clause is owed when a real, distinct next step exists and the named party can change the outcome (later reused to reject "file an issue" in FLLWUP-7). **Item 2** — key-based reason→message table with English defaults living in tunnel.ts; localization deferred to its own card (became [[FLLWUP-4 Ruling]]). **Item 3** — the `already connected` no-op is a stated refusal sentence, never a glyph — the anchor of the [[Copy Honesty Doctrine]]. **Item 4** — footer-merge policy deferred to EV-8 with a non-binding preference (highest-severity-wins), later self-amended by EV-8 J1; the binding parts (severity tags shipped, EV-8 owns the rule) survive.
