Five rulings on FLLWUP-4 (2026-09-02). **OJ1** — `ALREADY_LIVE_COPY` keying is a follow-up card, not a fold-in: copy ruled verbatim by a prior ruling changes only through its own card and ruling (see [[Stable Keys]]). **OJ2** — 22-key id table (16 mandated + 6 command-output rows); 50 rejected as re-introducing the unverified-non-native-Bahasa risk; partial coverage announced at the module surface, including the keyless `inputPrompt` literal. **OJ3** — locale source is env-over-setting (`PI_REMOTE_LOCALE` → `piRemote.locale` → `"en"`), matching the entry-point precedent. **OJ4** — the `urlExpired` remedy names `/rc` (re-dial is POST /tunnels, not re-enrollment), and the English row gains the named command in the same pass. **OJ5** — `<serverUrl>` substitution is in-card at the two re-pointed sites. General rules generalize [[Fixture-Green Honesty]] and [[Stable Keys]] to the whole Backlog (FLLWUP-11..16).
