## Intent

The assembly card — the one that makes the document a single coherent artifact
and enforces the self-containment mandate mechanically. It integrates the
sections in document order, adds the table of contents and reading paths, and
runs two audits:

**Self-containment audit (the mandate):** zero references anywhere in the
document to `docs/PI-SPEC.md`, to pi-remote source files or module names, to
card ids or internal decision records; exactly one external link
(https://github.com/jumpseat-inc/pi-remote), in the conformance framing
section. A grep-level check plus a read-through for soft references ("as the
client does", "see the host spec") — the document stands alone.

**Conformance audit (council-side, recorded on the card — never on the page):**
every wire contract in the document is checked against the shipped client's
behavior (its fixtures, its `parseInbound` union, its error handling) and every
client-facing obligation the client codes against is present in the document.
Additionally, PI-SPEC §10 gains the one-line pointer to
docs/SERVER-SIDE-SPEC.md (PI-SPEC is otherwise unchanged — the sync rule cuts
both ways). The card's acceptance is the epic's: a server team reading only
this document can implement a conformant server.

## Acceptance

- docs/SERVER-SIDE-SPEC.md exists as one integrated document with a table of
  contents and the document map.
- The self-containment audit passes: zero PI-SPEC/codebase/decision-id
  references, exactly one external link (the repo URL).
