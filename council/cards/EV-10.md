## Intent

The enrollment contract exists as shipped client behavior; this card writes the
server's side of it **on the page, in full** — nothing may be left to "as the
client expects". Covered: the discovery document (`authorization_endpoint`,
`token_endpoint`, `device_authorization_endpoint` required; `revocation_endpoint`
optional), attended authorization-code + PKCE with loopback redirect, headless
device flow with `interval`/`slow_down`/`authorization_pending`/`expired_token`/
`access_denied` semantics, refresh as `grant_type=refresh_token` at
token_endpoint (no separate /refresh), the `pi-remote:host` scope, and the token
claim contract (tenant-scoped `sub`). The error semantics must be stated with
their client-consumed meanings: 401 = invalid/expired/revoked credential (the
client's retry-with-re-enroll case), 403 = valid-but-insufficient-scope (the
client's distinct contact-admin case) — the two are never merged.

**Self-containment requirement:** every request/response shape, status code,
and error body appears complete on the page; the council verifies the section
against the client's enrollment fixtures (the in-repo mock IdP is reference
behavior), but the page cites none of that.

## Acceptance

- Every endpoint the client calls is specified with complete request/response
  shapes, status codes, and error bodies.
- Both grant flows are specified step by step, including the device-flow error
  codes the client's driver handles by name.
- Token claims and scope semantics are stated fully on the page.
- Council-side check (card record, not the page): the section passes the
  client's enrollment fixtures with nothing the client sends unspecified and
  nothing the page specifies the client contradicts.
